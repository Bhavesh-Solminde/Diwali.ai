## Training data and labels

Target: BTCUSDT USD-margined perpetuals, one-minute candles, next-candle prediction recursively extended to 15 minutes. Use one venue consistently for the first version. Do not silently join spot candles to perpetual books.

1. **Simulator pretraining:** independently generated matching-engine trades, visible depth updates, cancellations, latent reserve orders, bounded participant inventories, and synthetic news/flow regimes. Begin with 100,000 training windows for profiling. Split whole episodes and parameter draws 80/10/10 into training, validation and simulator test; never distribute branches of a common saved state between splits. Keep an additional stress suite with cancellation, reserve and news parameters outside the training ranges. Reserve state is engine truth and a diagnostic label, not an observable input to the deployable model.
2. **Real candle/trade training:** acquire and checksum-verify Binance perpetual OHLCV and trade archives for 2020–2023. Validation: calendar 2024. Historical test: calendar 2025. Additional locked chronological audit: 1 January–31 October 2026. These are proposed acquisition ranges, not a claim that these files have already been downloaded or verified. Release of 2025–2026 applies only to this BTC study.
3. **Public order-book training:** record matching-venue depth snapshots plus sequenced updates and executed trades, with exchange and local receipt timestamps. Resynchronize on sequence gaps. Historical candles cannot reconstruct actual historical books. Existing January 2023 book data with unverified venue identity remain an auxiliary benchmark, not a verified pairing with Binance perpetual candles.
4. **First prospective book dataset:** collect at least 90 usable calendar days; freeze days 1–60 for fitting, 61–75 for validation/calibration, and 76–90 for testing. A day is usable only after quality checks; record exclusions rather than hiding them. This initial test is limited regime coverage, not a permanent profitability certificate. Shorter runs are engineering checks only. Acquisition and recording require no exchange trading keys.
5. **News:** synthetic scheduled and unscheduled positive/negative/neutral events for simulator experiments. For real data, begin with timestamped CPI releases and recorded headlines. Only use consensus expectations where a genuine historical, pre-release source exists. Store publication/receipt time and revisions. Missing news features remain explicitly missing; do not retrospectively assign sentiment from the subsequent price move. Real news-conditioned training is gated on obtaining usable point-in-time history.
6. **Hidden-pressure estimates:** derive replenishment rate, executed-volume versus displayed-depth discrepancy, absorption, and uncertainty from observations available up to each forecast. These are estimates, not known hidden totals. The simulator can generate known hidden reserves and train an estimator, but real-market accuracy of that estimator remains unverified without suitable labels.

Existing assets: 131,040 BTC spot one-minute candles from January–March 2024, the candle prototype, and the previous order-book benchmark. These support engineering and comparison; they do not constitute the proposed complete perpetual training dataset.

Keep two evaluation tracks: the historical candle/trade model is fitted only on the historical training period; the full book-conditioned model is fitted and tested on the later prospective book periods. A model fine-tuned on 2026/2027 observations cannot claim 2025 as a chronological unseen test. Calibrate simulator parameters only from the training period of the relevant track.

At each forecast origin use 1,440 completed minute candles, the last 60 seconds of public depth/trade features, known news and calendar information, and explicit missing-data masks. Targets are the next OHLCV candle, later recursive horizons, and trade-level target/stop/timeout outcomes. Future events and complete future books are target-generation information only.

Include cyclic time-of-day and day-of-week features, plus explicitly timezone-aware session indicators. BTC remains a 24/7 target; evaluate session versus off-session performance rather than declaring off-hours invalid. Any session filter must be chosen using validation data. Optional contemporaneous ETH/SOL/XRP/DOGE features require synchronized historical observations and a separate ablation. Never use a future cross-asset move as a feature. The 15-minute rollout is our initial project horizon, not a verified claim about every later Tiger version; retain one-minute scoring and add a separately labeled 60-minute secondary experiment if required for the accuracy video's narrated horizon.

## Model candidates and computing environment

Start with an MLP: a multilayer perceptron is a neural network with successive fully connected layers. Proposed hidden widths are 256, 128 and 64, with nonlinear activations and validation-selected regularization. It receives the flattened, scaled fixed history and public features. Generate valid price/body/wick/volume coordinates and a distribution, not only one deterministic price. For a multi-step rollout, sample a generated candle and feed it back to predict the following candle; never train against the model's predictions as if they were observed truth. At the original forecast time, later real order books, trades and news are unavailable. Therefore the rollout must use an explicitly masked/frozen origin context or separately simulated future flow, never actual future book updates. Validate that rollout regime separately. The primary one-minute forecast always uses the latest observed inputs. Any direct multi-horizon alternative is a separately versioned candidate.

Compare with the existing causal sequence-model approach and simple last-close, rolling-candle and order-imbalance baselines. Give competing learned models the same information cutoff, input history, target definitions, data splits and tuning budget. The 1,440-minute sequence model must be profiled separately; the earlier 256-context timing does not establish its training time. Select architecture using validation results, not preference for the term MLP or transformer.

Local environment: existing Python/PyTorch environment on the Apple M5, 32 GB memory, MPS GPU for training and CPU reference checks. Pin dependency versions; save seeds, configuration, data hashes, checkpoint and preprocessing state. Run three fixed seeds (17, 42, 101). Start at batch 16; lower it if needed. Set a 12 GB process-memory target and benchmark a representative pilot before scaling. No cloud purchase is assumed.

Fit scalers only on training data. Perform early stopping and architecture selection on validation data. Use January–September 2024 for model selection and October–December 2024 for probability calibration. For the prospective book track, use days 61–70 for selection and 71–75 for calibration. Purge training targets that cross a split boundary; use a 15-minute target embargo. Earlier observations may legitimately provide context to a later forecast. Never shuffle timestamps across partitions.

## The three error panels

Use Krafer's three reporting groups, with explicit formulas and provenance. Also retain an internal validation panel.

| Panel | Data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Training error | Windows used for fitting; simulator and real data reported separately | Measure fitting progress; low error alone does not show generalization |
| Past unseen-data error | Locked chronological test period | Evaluate generalization after model and thresholds are frozen |
| Recent-market error | Latest 24 hours of completed forecast outcomes, plus 7-day context | Monitor current performance and regime deterioration |

Before prospective forecasts exist, label the recent panel **recent historical replay**, not live track record. A 15-minute forecast cannot be scored until its outcome matures. Show pending counts and the number of scored origins. The recent-error number evaluates earlier predictions; it is not the input that predicts the next candle.

Use these same reproducible calculations in each panel:

- Close MAE in USDT and basis points relative to the close at forecast origin.
- Close MSE and RMSE, and standardized HLCV MSE, with train/validation epoch curves. MSE is mean squared prediction error, not a percentage correct. Standardize using training-only scales and retain the scale metadata; report raw-price close MSE in squared USDT and RMSE in USDT. Do not compare values from differently scaled plots.
- Separate high, low, open and volume errors.
- Combined HLCV score: arithmetic mean of absolute errors in log(H/origin-close), log(L/origin-close), log(C/origin-close), and log(1+base-volume), divided by the respective training-set standard deviations with denominator floor 1e-8. This is our documented score; its values are not directly comparable to Krafer's undocumented scale.
- Report each horizon 1–15 separately; distinguish next-step predictions with real observed history from closed-loop recursive predictions.
- Direction accuracy and balanced accuracy with an explicit ±1 basis-point neutral band; show class counts and confusion matrix.
- Distribution quality: probabilistic loss, interval width and observed coverage for 50%, 80% and 90% intervals.
- Entry probabilities: Brier/log loss and reliability plots for target-first, stop-first and timeout. If the target and stop are both crossed within a candle, use underlying trades to establish order or label the outcome ambiguous; never assume a win.

Add a separately labeled Krafer-inspired move-fulfillment panel. For each predeclared horizon and side, divide realized favorable excursion from origin by the predicted favorable excursion; exclude and count zero/invalid predicted excursion denominators. Report upward/downward results separately, unclipped ratios and a clipped-to-[0,1] average, plus shares reaching 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%. Freeze aggregation and minimum excursion rules before testing. This is our explicit metric, not a proven reproduction of the creator's undocumented weighting. A reached price is not a successful trade unless target-before-stop, executable fills and costs are checked. Any outcome-conditioned directional subset is diagnostic only; it cannot select trades in advance.

Do not convert an error score into accuracy by subtracting it from 100, and do not require zero training error. Very low training error alongside worse unseen-data error is an overfitting warning.

## Paper-trading evaluation and viewer

Preserve hollow forecast candles, actual candles, forecast-origin markers, public heatmap, news markers, and immutable prediction history. Display all three error panels with dataset dates, horizon, formula and sample counts.

Evaluate both long and short entries. Initial stops: one 14-minute ATR; targets: two ATR. Waiting candidates use the strongest nearby visible liquidity zone within two ATR. A wait is a price alert, not a guaranteed limit fill. Reassess on arrival and create a new versioned entry decision using the new observations. A price that never arrives is an untriggered opportunity, not a win or loss.

Score entry/exit at executable bid/ask after a baseline 250 ms latency. Initial research assumptions: 5 basis points taker fee per side plus 2 basis points adverse slippage per side, in addition to spread; these are test assumptions, not a verified account fee schedule. Incorporate observed funding if a trade crosses a funding timestamp. Stress latency at 1 second and double the fee/slippage assumptions. Where historical bid/ask or funding are absent, label the result approximate and exclude it from claims of verified executable profitability.

Report net expected return, realized net return, trade count, win rate, payoff ratio, profit factor, drawdown, exposure, timeout outcomes, and untriggered waiting opportunities. Evaluate continuous chronological paper trading with one position at a time and existing project risk limits, separately from independent hypothetical opportunity scoring.

An entry is favorable only when estimated return after costs is positive and uncertainty/calibration support it. Otherwise show unclear/skip or unfavorable. Simulation-only estimates must retain that label. A displayed 80% target probability must be tested against subsequent outcomes; it cannot be manufactured from a hand-drawn cloud or by excluding timeout cases.

Freeze ten illustrative scenarios before evaluating their outcomes: bullish news, bearish news, neutral news, upward trend without news, downward trend without news, quiet range, thin liquidity, persistent wall, wall cancellation, and hidden replenishment. Real examples of inferred hidden liquidity must be labeled inferred. The main conclusion uses the complete eligible test set, not these ten examples.

Compare candle-only, book/trade-only, combined, combined-plus-news, and combined-plus-hidden-estimate variants. Use paired errors and block-bootstrap uncertainty by day. Require improved unseen forecasting and positive net paper-trading evidence under baseline and stressed costs before claiming an actionable edge. If results fail, deliver the failure and its measured causes rather than a profitability claim.

Retraining, feature changes, threshold changes or model switching based on test/live outcomes create a new model version. They require a new later test interval. Recent performance can monitor a frozen model; any adaptive selection policy must itself be tested chronologically using only outcomes already known at each decision.

## What hidden-liquidity feeds can actually supply

[CME MBO](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/faqs/market-by-order-mbo.html) supplies anonymous individual order information and can preserve an order ID when a native iceberg replenishes. This supports detection; it does not grant the complete BTC market's hidden reserve. CME futures are also a different instrument/venue from Binance BTC perpetuals.

[Bookmap's CME indicator documentation](https://bookmap.com/knowledgebase/docs/Addons-Stops-And-Icebergs-On-Chart-Indicator) explicitly states that an iceberg's remaining quantity at cancellation is unknown. Its [iceberg tracker documentation](https://bookmap.com/knowledgebase/es/docs/KB-Bookmap-Wiki-Iceberg-Orders-Tracker) describes estimation from event dynamics. Paid order-flow products therefore must be assessed for what they observe versus infer; subscription alone does not provide the hidden total.

Revised input rule: use any legitimate, timestamped observation or clearly labeled estimate available at forecast time. The simulator may contain complete hidden reserves internally. The deployed model must not depend on privileged quantities unavailable in the live feed. An oracle model with simulator-only full information may be evaluated separately as an upper-bound experiment, never reported as live-capable performance.

No automatic scaling above 20 million parameters in this first study.
6. **Freeze before testing.** Record architecture, preprocessing, seed/checkpoint selection, forecast horizon, probability calibration, minimum excursion, entry thresholds, costs and ten scenario-selection rules. 7. **Deliver paper evaluation and viewer.** Publish prediction-versus-actual charts for all ten cases, full-test aggregate results, calibration, cost sensitivity, failure examples and immutable prediction logs. 8. **Forward validation.** Continue frozen-model paper forecasts on newly arriving data. At least 30 calendar days is an initial operational check, with all trade/sample counts and confidence intervals disclosed; duration alone does not establish an edge. No live execution is included. The full book model's first held-out result also depends on completing the prospective collection split, unless suitable verified historical book data become available.

### Decision rule and final deliverables

The preferred forecast model must beat the strongest same-information baseline on predeclared next-candle probabilistic loss, with paired day-block confidence intervals reported; check close MAE, direction and interval coverage for material regressions. Treat trading usefulness as a separate decision: the frozen entry policy must have positive net paper expectancy under base and stressed costs, with uncertainty reported. If intervals include zero, label the result inconclusive rather than profitable. Limited data or absent bid/ask prohibit a verified executable-profit claim. Report forecast quality and trade-policy quality separately even when different models win.

Deliver: source-review ledger; versioned data manifest; simulator and quality checks; reproducible training configuration and checkpoints; full-test comparison and ten scenario charts; the three error panels plus internal validation; entry calibration/cost report; and a paper-only prediction viewer. The plan promises a measured result, including failure or no-trade, not a target win rate or income.

Completed development comparison

All 18 planned training runs are complete. The matched one-second model won the price-range comparison; the detailed event models did not improve it. Up/down prediction remains a weakness.

MethodPrice-range error ↓Up/down error ↓Up/down accuracy

Lower probability error is better. Each of three time periods has equal weight, and neural rows average two training seeds. Accuracy is not a trading win rate. A constant 50–50 direction forecast has error 0.69315. The historical-frequency baseline can be stronger than 50–50 when outcomes are uneven.

What remains unproven

Only periods passing the data checks are included. Historical provider timing is an assumption; full hidden liquidity is unavailable. Overlapping forecasts are related observations. A later live test is still needed, and these results do not establish perfect probabilities or profitability.
